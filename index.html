Juyeong Hwang

Ph.D. Student · IIIXR Lab, Korea University · Seoul

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Korea University, advised by Prof. HyeongYeop Kang in the IIIXR Lab. I received my B.S. in Software Convergence (Game Contents Track) from Kyung Hee University.

My research focuses on natural virtual agent behavior, spanning crowd and multi-agent simulation, motion synthesis, and vision-language-action agents. I want to create technology that brings joy to people.
